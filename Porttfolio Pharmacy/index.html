01 The company

A small company with a long horizon.

“We would rather run one honest trial than ship ten hopeful ones.” Marta Aebi, Chief Executive

Bioverse was founded in Basel in 2009 by three chemists who had spent a decade on the same stubborn target. We are still privately held, still in the same building, and still measured by whether the data hold up.
